## LinkedIn post (English)

New design. New style. My portfolio just got a full rebuild 🚀

I didn't just change the colours. I rebuilt it from the ground up:

✦ A 3D particle hero built with Three.js: thousands of particles that form my HM logo, then a laptop, then a </> icon
✦ A new brand identity: a circuit-style monogram, app icons and social banners
✦ A real backend: a contact API with server-side validation and a private admin dashboard
✦ Built end-to-end with React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind, deployed on Vercel

My favourite detail: the particles burst apart and reform into a new shape every few seconds, and the whole scene pauses when you scroll away so it doesn't waste your battery.

Take a look and tell me what you think. Feedback is very welcome 👇
🔗 houseinmonzer.vercel.app

I'm open to freelance projects and full-time roles.

## Posting tips

- LinkedIn tends to show posts with links in the body to fewer people. Post without the link and put the URL in the first comment.
- Instagram story: upload the story image, add a **Link** sticker pointing to houseinmonzer.vercel.app and place it over the "See it live" button.
- Keep the top and bottom ~250px of the story free of stickers so they don't cover the headline or the app's reply bar.
